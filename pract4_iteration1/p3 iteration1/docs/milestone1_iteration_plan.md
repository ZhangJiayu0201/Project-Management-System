# Milestone 1 - Three Iteration Plan

Project: Project Management System

The user stories were allocated into three iterations according to priority and estimated effort.

## Iteration 1

| Target Users | Title | Priority | Estimate | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Student Team Members | View My Assigned Tasks | 10 | 5 days | Done |
| Project Managers | Assign Team Tasks | 10 | 5 days | Todo |
| Student Team Members | Add New Tasks | 20 | 3 days | Done |

Total estimate: 13 days

## Iteration 2

| Target Users | Title | Priority | Estimate | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Student Team Members | Edit Task Information | 20 | 3 days | Todo |
| Project Managers | Track Project Progress | 20 | 4 days | Todo |
| Project Managers | Identify Delayed Tasks | 20 | 4 days | Todo |

Total estimate: 11 days

## Iteration 3

| Target Users | Title | Priority | Estimate | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Workplace Team Members | Share Task Updates | 30 | 3 days | Todo |
| Workplace Team Members | Remove Completed Tasks | 30 | 2 days | Todo |

Total estimate: 5 days

## Rationale

Iteration 1 focuses on baseline functionality: viewing assigned tasks, creating new tasks, and preparing task assignment. Iteration 2 focuses on project control and progress visibility. Iteration 3 focuses on communication and cleanup features.
